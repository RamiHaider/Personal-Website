Original Upload: 2025-01-25

Interactive Map

Explore mineral potential predictions across Quebec using the interactive map below. Toggle between different mineral layers and click regions for detailed analysis.

How it works

  • Drag and Zoom to the Area you want to predict
  • Click 'Predict Region' Button
  • Click on the Area you want to predict
  • Scroll down to see the Results
  • *Please note, some areas do not have data, and the statistics will show as Null or Infinity. Look at the heatmap maps to get ani dea of the available regions to predict
